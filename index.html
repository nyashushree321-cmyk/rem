About Our Organisation

REM – Rarest Earth Metals is an institutional-grade enterprise engineered to drive India's critical mineral independence and clean-tech revolution.

Critical elements—including the 15 lanthanides, plus scandium and yttrium—are the foundational catalyst for electric mobility (EVs), wind energy turbines, defense aerospace, and semiconductor electronics. REM approaches this high-stakes landscape with comprehensive supply chain transparency, disciplined engineering, and ethical governance.

  • Strategic Minerals Vision: Securing sustainable supply chains for green transition.

Lawful & Transparent

Insurance Claim Policy & Workflow

REM assists clients with lawful, documented insurance claims under strict Indian statutory and regulatory guidelines. Integrity and truthfulness guide every case.

Our 5-Stage Structured Claim Process

1

Dossier Intake

Case examination, policy review and initial document verification.

2

Technical Audit

Verification of authenticity, loss assessment and valuation records.

3

Filing & Liaison

Lawful submission to surveyor and ongoing insurer communication.

4

Status Updates

Transparent stage-by-stage communication provided directly to client.

5

Formal Closure

File completed upon formal insurer decision and final settlement.

Zero Tolerance for Fraud

We process only verifiable, lawful claims. Any inflated or fabricated files are immediately refused.

Insurer Autonomy

Final settlement decisions rest solely with licensed insurance companies. We guarantee procedural rigor, not arbitrary approval.

Strict Confidentiality

All sensitive valuation certificates, personal records, and financial papers remain encrypted and private.
